Species Information: Pseudomonas aeruginosa ATCC BAA-3197 is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped, non-spore-forming, motile, oxidase-positive, obligately aerobic bacterium that exhibits versatile metabolic capabilities, including oxidative metabolism and robust biofilm formation, which contribute to its persistence in both clinical and environmental settings.

Unique Mutations: This strain harbors the blaKPC-2 gene on a small, 3,652 bp plasmid pBH6 featuring Tn3 resolvase and inverted repeats along with a partial ISKpn6 element but lacking rep genes, enabling production of KPC-2 carbapenemase that hydrolyzes β-lactam antibiotics. ￼ ￼

Antibiotic and antimicrobial peptides Resistance: The strain exhibits high-level resistance to multiple β-lactams including cefazolin (MIC ≥ 64 μg/mL), cefepime (MIC ≥ 64 μg/mL), cefotaxime (MIC ≥ 64 μg/mL), ceftazidime (MIC ≥ 64 μg/mL), piperacillin (MIC ≥ 128 μg/mL), piperacillin/tazobactam (MIC ≥ 128 μg/mL), imipenem (MIC ≥ 16 μg/mL), meropenem (MIC ≥ 16 μg/mL), and doripenem (MIC ≥ 8 μg/mL) primarily via enzymatic hydrolysis by KPC-2 carbapenemase encoded by blaKPC-2. The strain is also resistant to fluoroquinolones such as ciprofloxacin (MIC ≥ 4 μg/mL), levofloxacin (MIC ≥ 8 μg/mL), and norfloxacin (MIC ≥ 16 μg/mL) due to target-site modifications in DNA gyrase (gyrA Thr83→Ile) and topoisomerase IV (parC Ser87→Leu) combined with overexpression of Mex efflux pumps. ￼

Antibiotic and antimicrobial peptides Sensitivity: The strain is susceptible to amikacin (MIC 8 μg/mL) through irreversible binding to the 30S ribosomal subunit resulting in inhibition of protein synthesis. The strain shows susceptible/intermediate susceptibility to gentamicin (MIC 4 μg/mL) via binding to helix 44 of 16S rRNA in the 30S subunit, causing mRNA misreading. The strain is susceptible to tobramycin (MIC ≤ 1 μg/mL) by preventing formation of the 70S initiation complex through binding to both the 30S and 50S ribosomal subunits.
